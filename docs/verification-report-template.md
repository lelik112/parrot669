- A status of CONFIRMED means the specific fact was supported by the reviewed evidence; it does not guarantee the entire transaction.
- PARROT 669 may not be able to independently verify every document, statement or relationship.
- Public or third-party information can be incomplete, delayed or incorrect.
- This report is not legal, tax, structural, technical or financial advice unless a separate appropriately qualified service is expressly engaged.
