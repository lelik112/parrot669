## 4. Material inconsistencies / data-quality flags

### DQ-01 — 1 m² floor area

Fotocasa displays **1 m²**. Multiple syndicated copies repeat the same value.

This is incompatible with the advertised one-bedroom apartment and the supplied photos, and the underlying Spotahome page does not present 1 m² as a meaningful apartment size.

**Interpretation:** likely syndication/default-field corruption, not evidence of fraud.

**Operational lesson:** area must be checked against the primary source or contract/property record rather than trusted from a syndicated portal.

### DQ-02 — Energy certificate values

Fotocasa/Habitaclia show **G / 999**, while Spotahome states that the energy certificate has been requested from the landlord.

**Interpretation:** likely placeholder/syndication data unless an actual certificate is produced.

**Operational lesson:** do not report portal energy fields as verified certificate data without seeing the certificate/source.

### DQ-03 — Important eligibility condition hidden downstream

The Fotocasa capture markets the property generally as temporary/student-friendly, but the underlying Spotahome listing states that the landlord **only accepts students** and requires university acceptance/enrollment evidence.

**Operational lesson:** for syndicated listings, always inspect the underlying source before the client pays or spends time progressing the application.

## 5. Platform-level observations

The exact Spotahome reference exists publicly and is consistently syndicated across several housing portals. Spotahome itself labels the landlord as verified/professional and displays platform-history/review signals.

These facts reduce the uncertainty around whether the **advertisement/source chain itself** is fabricated, but they do **not** independently prove:

- the legal identity of the landlord;
- ownership of the property;
- authority to rent it;
- exact address/unit;
- correctness of the future contract;
- correctness of future payment instructions.

3. **Treat platform "verified landlord" badges as platform claims, not as our own verification.**
4. **Never trust obviously machine-propagated structured fields without cross-checking them.**
5. **Eligibility, deposit and payment rules may exist only on the underlying source.**
